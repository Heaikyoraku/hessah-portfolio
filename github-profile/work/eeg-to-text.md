# EEG-to-Text Research

### What Actually Matters in EEG-to-Text Decoding?

**Research internship · KACST · 2026**  
**ICDR 2026 poster:** *A Comprehensive Lobe, Frequency, and Language Analysis*

Exploration of which aspects of an EEG-to-text pipeline matter most for decoding performance: **brain-region coverage**, **signal frequencies**, and **language transfer**, including Arabic.

## Stack & methods

![Tools](https://skillicons.dev/icons?i=py,pytorch&theme=dark)

**EEG Processing** · **Deep Learning** · **LLMs** · **Spatial Ablation** · **Frequency Analysis**

## Research outcome

The experiments reported **56.30% classification accuracy**, compared with the reference result of **53.0%**. The percentage is a classification benchmark within the research pipeline; it does not mean 56.30% word-for-word thought transcription.

The poster was presented at the seventh International Conference on Disability and Rehabilitation Research (ICDR 2026).
