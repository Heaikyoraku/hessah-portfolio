### Research notes

**EEG-to-Text · KACST (2026)** — Reproduced and extended an EEG-to-text research pipeline, studying electrode selection and frequency bands, with experiments in Arabic generation. Reached **56.30% classification accuracy** against a **53.0% reported baseline**. The research was presented as a poster at **ICDR 2026**.

**Emora · Graduation project (2026)** — A multimodal emotion-recognition and Arabic generative storytelling system using EEG and eye tracking, developed with my team. Evaluated across **7 emotion classes** using leave-one-subject-out validation.
