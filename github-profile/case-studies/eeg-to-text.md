# EEG-to-Text: decoding, frequency, space, and language

**Research internship — King Abdulaziz City for Science and Technology (KACST), 2026**

**Poster:** *What Actually Matters in EEG-to-Text Decoding? A Comprehensive Lobe, Frequency, and Language Analysis*  
**Presented:** 7th International Conference on Disability and Rehabilitation Research (ICDR 2026), Riyadh.

## Research question

What changes in EEG-to-text performance when we vary signal frequency bands, the spatial selection of electrodes, and the language of generated text?

## Research overview

The work investigated a deep learning and language-model pipeline for generating text from EEG representations, with experiments focused on:

1. **Spatial coverage:** Contributions of selected brain regions and reduced electrode configurations.
2. **Frequency bands:** How different EEG spectral components affect decoding performance.
3. **Language transfer:** Extending experiments beyond English, with attention to Arabic.

## Reported outcome

Experiments reached **56.30% classification accuracy**, compared with a **53.0% result from the referenced baseline**. These values describe a research evaluation task; they are **not** a measure of full natural-language transcription accuracy.
